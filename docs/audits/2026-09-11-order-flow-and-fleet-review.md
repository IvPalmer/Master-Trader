The paper studies signed buying and selling activity across 82 cryptocurrencies from January 2018 through June 2022. Its daily sample excludes weekends and US holidays; its weekly observations include seven days. The 82-coin panel requires continuous nonzero trading throughout the sample and market capitalization above $1 million at its end. That selection excludes failed or discontinued assets using future information. It is a survivorship-selected research universe, not a point-in-time investable portfolio. The recursive forecast evaluation runs from **February 18, 2020 through June 30, 2022**, following separate training and validation periods and monthly updating. These distinctions matter when interpreting its results. See paper pages 9–11 and 20–21.

Its order flow is the log difference between buyer-initiated and seller-initiated transaction volume, standardized by recent flow volatility. CryptoCompare supplies signed volume across more than 300 exchanges and eleven fiat currencies: the G10 currencies plus Korean won. This is different information from candle direction, total volume, open interest, funding rates, or an order-book imbalance snapshot. Binance USDT flow could test a related mechanism, but would not reproduce the paper's international currency structure.

The strongest practical insight is the distinction between daily and weekly signals. At the daily horizon, raw aggregate flow produced a weak long-short sort; removing the component associated with same-period returns improved the result. The paper constructs that residual recursively using only information available at the decision time. Weekly raw flow worked more convincingly without this adjustment. This favors starting with two simple, separately registered hypotheses: daily residual flow and weekly raw flow. It does not favor immediately searching a large machine-learning model family. See pages 26–27 and Table 7.

The proposed **OrderFlowResidualRankV1** is a research plan only; no strategy or collector is being deployed:

1. **Build a cheap, auditable flow dataset.** Binance spot klines expose total quote volume and taker-buy quote volume. Let buyer-initiated quote volume be `B` and seller-initiated volume be `S = total_quote_volume - B`; test `log(B) - log(S)` with a prespecified zero-volume policy. Retain raw fields, close times, retrieval times and quality flags. Never infer transaction direction from a green/red candle. This is a single-venue USDT proxy, not G11 world flow. Validate the decomposition against aggregate trades on a small sample. The fields are documented in [Binance's market-data API](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/market-data-endpoints).

2. **Register two simple baselines.** Daily: rank the component of standardized flow unexplained by recent returns, with regression coefficients and scaling estimated only on permitted training/history. Weekly: rank raw standardized flow. Also retain return-only and equal-weight-universe controls. Freeze the normalization, residualization window, missing-data rule, ranking and holding period before inspecting the final holdout. Keep model-family search out of the first experiment.

3. **Preserve causal timing and investability.** Use closed UTC bars and trade only after their data could have arrived. Store historical universe membership, listing/delisting dates and liquidity eligibility; do not select today's survivors retrospectively. Specify how suspended markets and delistings exit. Explicitly distinguish a seven-day crypto calendar from the paper's business-day daily sample. Fit preprocessing only on training data, use separate validation for choices, and preserve an untouched chronological test period. Rolling refits must follow the frozen schedule.

4. **Evaluate a portfolio, not just predictions.** Measure net returns, drawdown including open marks, turnover, exposures, concentration and confidence intervals. Compare long-only results with equal-weight exposure to the same available universe and with market/return controls, so beta is not called order-flow skill. A long-short diagnostic is optional and must not assume unavailable shorts, free borrowing, or absent funding. Reproduce actual slots, capital, quantity precision and order minima. Stress fee tiers, spreads, slippage, delayed arrivals, maker nonfills and order timeouts; use detail bars where exits depend on intrabar paths.

5. **Keep the decision falsifiable.** Require improvement over frozen controls after realistic costs across multiple chronological market regimes, not just a positive gross Sharpe or one profitable slice. Reject the candidate if the result disappears with realistic fills, delisted assets, or modest timing delay. Only then consider an explicitly bounded shadow observation period; live admission would require a separate review.
